10 Question Practice Quiz

Question 1 of 10
A neural network produces an incorrect prediction during training. Which combination of processes enables the network to learn from this error and update its weights?
The loss function measures the prediction error, and backpropagation calculates gradients that guide the adjustment of network weights.
Question 2 of 10
Which prompting method gives an AI model several examples so that it can infer the expected pattern before handling a new input?
Few-shot prompting places a small number of examples in the prompt to demonstrate the expected task pattern.
Question 3 of 10
In a RAG system, what is the main purpose of searching a vector database after the user's query has been converted into a suitable representation?
The vector search identifies the most semantically relevant stored knowledge chunks, which can then be supplied as context to the language model.
Question 4 of 10
What distinguishes Supervised Fine-Tuning or Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning from ordinary Prompt Engineering?
Fine-tuning uses training data to modify model parameters or parameter-efficient adapters, while prompt engineering changes how the model is instructed without retraining its parameters.
Question 5 of 10
Which statement best explains why an LLM can sometimes respond with information that sounds convincing but is actually incorrect?
LLMs generate text from learned statistical patterns and do not inherently guarantee that every generated statement is factually correct.
Question 6 of 10
Which design choice can help make an LLM response more reliable when the model may not have enough information to answer a factual question?
Relevant context gives the model information to work with, while an instruction to acknowledge uncertainty discourages unsupported fabrication.
Question 7 of 10
Hidden layers in a neural network perform an important transformation between the input and output. What do they primarily help the model learn?
Hidden layers transform information and learn increasingly complex representations and feature relationships from the input.
Question 8 of 10
If two different LLMs are given exactly the same task, which factors should be considered when evaluating their responses?
Meaningful LLM comparison should focus on the quality of the generated responses, including accuracy, relevance, explanation, creativity where appropriate, and usefulness.
Question 9 of 10
A lecturer has a dataset containing marks for 20 students. Which task could an AI-assisted analysis tool perform on this dataset?
AI-assisted data analysis can calculate statistics such as averages and identify records such as the highest-scoring student.
Question 10 of 10
Before publishing an AI-generated poster for a college awareness campaign, what should the user do?
Responsible use requires reviewing AI-generated images for potential bias, copyright concerns, misleading content, and inappropriate representation before publication.
